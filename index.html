Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?
A Government of India scheme providing subsidies to residential households for rooftop solar. Eligible homeowners receive up to ₹78,000 directly in their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts to offset installation costs.
How much subsidy will I get in West Bengal?
1kW → ₹30,000. 2kW → ₹60,000. 3kW and above → ₹78,000 (maximum cap). The 3kW system is the sweet spot — maximum subsidy with the best per-rupee return.
How much will I save on my monthly electricity bill?
A 3kW system saves ₹3,000–₹4,000/month depending on your tariff slab. Over 25 years, total savings exceed ₹10 lakhs even accounting for rising tariffs.
Will I have power during load shedding?
Standard on-grid systems shut down during cuts (anti-islanding safety). For backup during outages — common in Nadia interior — opt for a hybrid system with battery. We provide both options.
How long does the complete process take?
Physical installation: 1–2 days. Full process with WBSEDCL net metering: 2–4 weeks. Subsidy credited 30–45 days after commissioning. We track your application status throughout.
Do panels work on cloudy days and monsoon?
Yes, at reduced efficiency — 20–30% on overcast days. WB's monsoon reduces output but annual average generation remains viable for positive ROI. All savings estimates factor this in.
